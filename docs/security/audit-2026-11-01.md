# Security & correctness audit — 2026-11-01 (finance, dashboards, tracking)

**Scope:** `2b1e480..ef42c6b`:
- finance AR core (migrations `20261031000100`, `0110`)
- dashboards (`0200`)
- work-hours tracking (`0300`, D-14)
- their screens, cron routes and integration edits

**Method:** an independent reviewer worked from the code alone. Every finding was reproduced against PostgreSQL 16, with four concurrent sessions for the allocation race.

**Result:** 10 findings, all confirmed, none High. **All fixed** in `20261101000100_audit3_finance_fixes.sql` and `20261101000200_audit3_tracking_fixes.sql`.

Each fix has a regression test that fails without it. This was checked by running suites 31 and 32 with the fix migration moved aside: 39 of 68 failed in suite 31 and 28 of 39 in suite 32.

## Found sound

- **Money:**
  - Amounts, VAT, due dates and numbers are computed on the server.
  - An invoice cannot be issued twice for the same fulfilled quantity, and idempotency keys are bound to their documents.
  - Pending and rejected payments never reach the ledger; reversals happen only once.
  - Balance always equals the ledger.
  - Documents are immutable and ledgers append-only.
  - Numbering stays gapless under rollback.
- **Credit-note approval:** no self-approval; amounts are re-checked under lock at decision time.
- **Scope:** finance reads follow customer scope, dashboards follow employee scope, and tracking follows the manager chain. No RLS cycles.
- **Tracking:**
  - Points are bound to the caller's own shift.
  - The notice is enforced at shift start.
  - No coordinates reach the audit log, outbox or notifications.
  - The collector runs only during an open shift.
  - Device state is cleared on sign-out and on a user switch.
- **Platform:**
  - All new definer functions use `search_path = ''`, require permissions and scope, and are revoked from `public`/`anon`.
  - The privilege sweep (suite 17) is still meaningful.
  - Cron routes refuse a missing secret and compare in constant time.
